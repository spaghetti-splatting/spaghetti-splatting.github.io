Orbit (MeshLab trackball)

The dot is the orbit point. The circle is the trackball.

Drag around the circle: roll

Double click / double tap: put the dot on a surface point

Right drag / Shift + drag / two fingers: pan (the dot moves too)

Wheel / pinch: zoom toward the dot

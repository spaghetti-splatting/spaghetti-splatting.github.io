The image is the colour-only evaluation render of the trained model, computed in WebGPU (a WGSL port of the CUDA rasterizer). Pulled hair keeps its trained segments: each segment moves rigidly with the simulated strand (DFTL), and its kernel frame and colour sites turn with it. A cut divides a segment at the cut point: each part keeps the trained parameters. The cut pieces fall and leave the scene 3 s after the cut at the latest. Reset brings back the trained hair. A head turn or a shake moves the head segments rigidly about the neck; the strand simulation moves the hair, with hair-hair friction.

Tutorial

Look around

  • Orbit (default): drag to turn the view about the pivot. Right drag or Shift + drag moves the view. The wheel or a pinch zooms to the cursor. A double click puts the pivot on that point.
  • Walk (Tab): W A S D move, Q and E move down and up, a drag turns the view.
  • ⌂ (R) puts the view back. ‹ › step through the camera views of the dataset. fov sets the field of view.
  • A drag that starts on the background always moves the view, also when a hair tool is on.

Grab (G)

  • Drag a strand. The lock under the cursor follows the cursor.
  • Release the button. The lock goes back into place.
  • lock size sets the size of the lock that you grab (1 to 10 cm).

Scissors (V)

  • Drag a straight line across the hair.
  • At the release, the scissors cut each strand where it crosses the line. The cut pieces fall.
  • Hair behind the head is not cut.

Clipper (C)

  • Select a guard (1.6 to 25.4 mm). The guard is the length of hair that stays on the head.
  • Drag over the head. The clipper cuts all hair that is longer than the guard, as electric hair clippers do.

Turn head (T)

  • Drag the head to turn it. The hair follows.
  • Release the button. The head swings back, and the hair swings with it.

Wind (B)

  • Push the button to start a wind from the left of the view. Push it again to stop the wind.

Shake

  • Push the button. The head shakes for 4.5 s.

Reset (X)

  • Push the button to put the hair, the cut hair and the head back.
